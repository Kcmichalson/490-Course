   ```
   python tools\package_mod.py --mods-dir "D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\XCOM 2\XCom2-WarOfTheChosen\XComGame\Mods" --version 0.8.2
   ```
2. **Laptop (Steam offline):** hash check, replace `Mods\X2TacticalCoop`

## B. Mission 1 (Skirmish, LAN): pause, hacks, objective
Pick a mission with a hackable **objective** (a workstation or computer
the squad must hack). Give the laptop a **Specialist**; give the PC a
Specialist too. If Skirmish lets you, add a **Skulljack** to a laptop
soldier and include **Spectres** in the enemies (for steps 15 and 17).
5. **PC:** CO-OP: HOST. **Laptop:** JOIN.
6. **Laptop pause, no soldiers:** on CO-OP: SQUAD give every soldier to
   the PC (or wait until all the laptop's soldiers are out of moves).
   On the laptop press **Escape**: the pause menu should open, and the
   laptop pauses (the PC keeps running). Its Save option should be greyed
   out or missing. Close it. ⏱ Give the laptop its soldiers back.
7. **PC hacks** (any hackable, or the objective with the PC's Specialist
   if you'd rather save it): on the laptop, try to click **Hack**, a
   **reward**, and **Cancel** on the copy it watches. Nothing should
   happen on the laptop. ⏱ The PC then chooses and hacks normally; both
   carry on after the result.
8. **Laptop's Specialist at the objective (repair OFF):** walk to the
   green tiles. Is there a hack icon? (Expected: no, as in 0.8.1.) ⏱
9. **Turn the repair ON (laptop):** CO-OP: FIXES → tick **Partner:
   re-link objects to the host's states**. End XCOM's turn; at the start
   of the next XCOM turn the laptop re-links. ⏱ Move the Specialist one
   tile and back onto the green tiles. Hack icon now? ⏱
10. **If the icon appears:** hack from the laptop, choose a reward. Both
    screens: the result plays, the objective updates on both, the game
    carries on. ⏱ On the PC, try clicking the copy it watches: nothing
    should happen there either.
11. **Laptop cancels** a hack with CO-OP: CANCEL HACK (if another hackable
    is left): both carry on.
