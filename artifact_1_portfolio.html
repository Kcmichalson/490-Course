Development Log (LOG.md)

# Artifact 1 Log
- Selected Model: Gemini 2.5 Flash with Extended Thinking (Free Web Chat).
- Iteration 1: Generated initial static layout. Identified cards were permanently extended and shelf was positioned too low on screen.
- Iteration 2: Re-anchored shelf to 46vh to provide equal stage space for upward card movement and downward water reflection.
- Iteration 3: Rebuilt slot stacking (sleeve z-index: 4, card z-index: 2) so cards slide smoothly out of the bins on hover.
- Iteration 4: Added dynamic background crossfading system across hover states.
