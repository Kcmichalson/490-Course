My campaign world has these powers and events. Using the map, decide where
each one belongs and why its climate fits.

Empires and peoples:
1. The Ashen Throne: a desert empire of salt mines and oasis cities.
2. The Verdant League: river city-states that grow rice and trade by boat.
3. The Frostwardens: herders and fur traders on the edge of the ice.
4. The Highmarch: a mountain kingdom guarding passes and silver mines.
5. The Tidewrights: a sea-trading republic with a mild, rainy capital.

Historical events:
6. The Long Drought: a famine that toppled a grain-growing dynasty.
7. The Burning of the Southern Archive: a library city destroyed in war.

Give one placement for each of the seven, then campaign hooks that connect
at least three of them.
