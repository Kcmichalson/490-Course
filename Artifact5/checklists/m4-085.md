## Part 1: first mission

   CO-OP.**
5. **PC:** at turn 1: Co-op icon, then HOST (STEAM). The overlay opens (no
   partner remembered yet); invite the friend. ⏱
6. **Friend:** accept from the Steam notification (or Shift+Tab, chat). The
   mission should load with no other click. ⏱ If a lobby or a message box
   appears instead, 📷 `m4-085-invite.png` and note the words.
7. **PC:** SQUAD: give the friend 2 soldiers, one a **Specialist**.
8. **Greyed targets** (whenever it comes up, any mission):
   - **Friend:** when an enemy shows in the target bar but greyed out, say
     so, note the soldier, and 📷 `m4-085-greyed.png` if possible. ⏱

   - If still greyed, **PC:** SQUAD, take that soldier, try the same shot,
     then give it back. ⏱
   - Note "never came up" if it doesn't.
9. **Objective hack:**
   - **Friend:** move the Specialist next to the objective (1 tile), keep it
     selected 3 s. Hack icon? ⏱
   - **PC:** hack an objective (or any hack) with a PC soldier while the
     friend watches. **Friend:** does the hack screen close, and can they
     play on? ⏱ If it sticks, wait 30 s before saying so.
10. **Lag:** play 10+ turns. **Friend:** say when alien turns look sped up
    (fast mode), and whether the screen still falls clearly behind. ⏱
11. **Friend, during an alien turn:** Esc (pause menu), then Options. Is
    **Zip Mode** shown as the friend normally has it (usually off)? Back
    out without changing anything. ⏱
12. **From turn 5:** both untick **Camera: follow moves (override)** on
    FIXES (keep the watcher ticked). Do cameras still follow every move? ⏱
13. **Mission end:** the friend gets **CO-OP: MISSION OVER** and returns to
    the main menu. ⏱
